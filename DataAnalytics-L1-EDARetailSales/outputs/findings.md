# Findings

## Dataset and cleaning
- Original dataset: 541,909 transaction lines.
- Exact duplicate rows identified: 0.
- Duplicate rows removed: 0.
- Cancelled invoices, negative quantities/returns, zero quantities, zero/negative prices, and invalid dates were excluded from sales-performance analysis.
- Clean analysis dataset: 530,104 positive-sales transaction lines.
- CustomerID is missing for 132,220 cleaned transaction lines.
- The dataset does not contain age or gender fields, so age-group and gender analysis is not fabricated.

## Sales trends
- Highest-revenue month: **2011-11**, revenue **1,509,496.33**.
- Highest-revenue quarter: **2011Q4**.
- Average revenue per invoice: **534.40**; median: **303.84**.

## Product and market concentration
- Leading product by units: **PAPER CRAFT , LITTLE BIRDIE**, with **80,995** units.
- United Kingdom revenue share: approximately **84.6%**.
- Top 10 countries revenue share: approximately **97.2%**.

## Actionable recommendations
1. Plan inventory, staffing, and campaigns around peak periods.
2. Protect high-performing products and use complementary bundles.
3. Prioritise major revenue markets while selectively testing international growth.
4. Improve CustomerID capture to strengthen retention, frequency, and lifetime-value analysis.
